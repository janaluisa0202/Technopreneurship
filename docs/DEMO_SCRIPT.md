# SakayWise local demonstration

All route, fare, wait and advisory values are illustrative. No live service is connected.

1. First launch: choose a language, Get Started, move through four introduction steps, and Start Commuting. Returning launches skip completion; Settings → View Introduction Again replays it.
2. Routes: choose UST Frassati (España) → SM North EDSA / Annex. Compare Fastest, Cheapest, Less Waiting and Fewest Transfers. Read the original sample signboards, wait/walk/transfer metrics and commute steps.
3. Queues: submit a High report for UST España, return to Routes and compare the changed wait/ranking. Submit Low to restore a lighter local queue. Reports expire after 30 minutes and affect this device only.
4. Fare Matrix: choose the passenger category. Compare a quoted amount with the selected route, or explicitly switch to the separate sample calculator. No legal overcharging determination is made.
5. Save a route and open the header bookmark control. View the saved boarding guide. This is stored locally, not offline GPS navigation.
6. Para Alerts: explore RoadWatch filters/details and My Alerts preferences. Watch the route. Preferences affect local highlights, not background notifications.
7. Expand Map: read the unavailable-state explanation and open commute steps. There is no geographic map or tile source in this version.
8. Settings: try Filipino, Dark mode and 130% text, then restore your preference. Help & Support opens seven FAQs and a message composer with a device-only notice. Demo messages are not sent to an administrator.

For deterministic RoadWatch clear/closure/no-alternative development scenarios, see [ROADWATCH_SETTINGS_SUPPORT.md](ROADWATCH_SETTINGS_SUPPORT.md).
